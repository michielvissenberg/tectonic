# Glossary

## Customer

The KBC account holder who is having a conversation with Kate and may request help from a human agent.

## Kate conversation

The ongoing customer interaction with Kate before, during, and immediately after a request for human help. The conversation is the source context for an escalation dossier.

## Human escalation

The customer-initiated transition from Kate to a human customer-service worker when the customer feels Kate cannot handle the issue. The escalation may continue in the chat or move to a call-center interaction.

## Escalation dossier

An internal KBC record created during human escalation. It contains a generated summary of the Kate conversation, the customer's unresolved question, relevant account context, and links to financial documents that Kate could already access.

## Relevant financial document

A financial document that the system selects because it helps a customer-service worker understand or resolve the customer's specific question. Relevance is determined from the existing Kate conversation and available account context.

## Customer-service worker

The KBC employee who opens an escalation dossier from the customer's account and continues helping the customer. The worker should be able to understand the situation without asking the customer to repeat the full conversation. In the POC, the worker can send a chat message; phone support is a future interaction mode.

## Escalation status

The customer-facing state of a human escalation, such as sent or waiting for customer service. It tells the customer that the request was transferred without exposing the internal dossier.

## Source context

The original Kate conversation and the account information and documents available to Kate. The escalation dossier must be grounded in this context rather than inventing new information.
